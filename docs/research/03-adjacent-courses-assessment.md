## Decision principle

Medicine (MBBS/MBChB, UCAS A100) is the primary product and the brand. Adjacent courses are included only where **(a)** a Nigerian student who wanted Medicine would realistically consider them as an alternative or stepping-stone, **(b)** international undergraduate (or PG) places genuinely exist, and **(c)** our Medicine-specific expertise (UCAT, interviews, personal statements, visa/finance for a long clinical course) transfers. Anything that fails (a) or (c) pushes the site toward being a generic study-abroad agency and is excluded regardless of demand.

## RECOMMENDATION — how to keep Medicine primary

1. Navigation: "Medicine" is the only top-level course item. Dentistry, Pharmacy and Biomed live under a single "Other routes" menu entry with a one-line disclaimer that Medicine is our specialism.
2. Every adjacent page opens with the decision the student is actually making ("If you cannot get a Medicine place…") rather than selling the course.
3. No adjacent page gets a per-university sub-cluster until Semrush data (when units exist) shows demand that the Medicine cluster cannot capture.
4. Before any adjacent page goes live, re-run the unrun searches listed at the top of this document and replace every `NOT VERIFIED THIS SESSION` / `DATA UNAVAILABLE` cell with a `VERIFY-ON-PAGE` fact and URL.
