## 3. University and official sources that rank — strengths/weaknesses for a Nigerian reader

Observed ranking: abdn.ac.uk (Nigeria entry), manchester.ac.uk (Nigeria entry requirements; MPH news), cardiff.ac.uk (Nigeria entry), ed.ac.uk (Nigeria UG and PG), st-andrews.ac.uk (Nigeria entry), nottingham.ac.uk (Nigeria country info), southampton.ac.uk (Nigeria equivalencies), kingston.ac.uk (Nigeria entry), shu.ac.uk (Nigeria entry), aru.ac.uk (Nigeria), bsms.ac.uk (fees), buckingham.ac.uk (PreMed PDF on a *staging* subdomain test1.), bma.org.uk (visa guides — owns "work after medicine"), ucat.ac.uk (UCAT Essentials — one appearance in a brand query only).

**Did not appear in any Part A result:** medschools.ac.uk (MSC), ucas.com, gmc-uk.org, gov.uk (visas), mdcn.gov.ng, pearsonvue.com/ucat, ncuk.ac.uk. INFERENCE: the most authoritative sources are invisible for Nigerian phrasing; a site that cites them per claim can "borrow" their authority while filling the Nigerian-language gap.

**What they do well:** state WASSCE/NECO = GCSE-level and that a recognised foundation or A-levels/IB is needed (Aberdeen snippet: medicine requires A-levels, IB or Highers); publish English-language rules (NECO C6 accepted by some); give PG equivalencies (GPA 3.5/5.0, 60%, 2:1).
**What they do poorly for a Nigerian reader (INFERENCE from snippets):** medicine is usually an exception buried in a generic country page; no naira costs; no UCAT-in-Nigeria logistics; no MDCN/return guidance; no cross-university comparison; fee pages are per-university and undated in snippets; one university exposes a staging URL in results (Buckingham) — poor hygiene signals low competition for this intent.

## 4. Gap analysis (INFERENCE — nothing below was observed as offered by any competitor)

1. **Per-university WAEC/NECO/A-level acceptance table for medicine** with the source URL and a "verified on" date for each row (the SERP currently contradicts itself: "many accept WAEC with A1/B2" vs "no school accepts WAEC alone").
2. **Verified-date international fee table** in GBP with dated NGN conversion, per school, pre-clinical vs clinical years (competitor figures conflict: Lincoln £28,700 yet home-only; Leicester £29,000 vs £30,150; "up to £32,000" stale).
3. **UCAT-in-Nigeria logistics page**: Pearson VUE centres (Lagos/Abuja), booking-window dates, slot-scarcity guidance, costs, what to do if no slot — currently one blog paragraph, no official page ranks.
4. **"Which foundation years lead to medicine" explainer** naming the routes mentioned in snippets (University of Lancashire MBBS Foundation Entry — international only; St Andrews International Foundation for Medicine; Buckingham MB ChB; NCUK IFY with Lagos/Abuja/Port Harcourt centres) with per-route eligibility, fees, progression conditions — VERIFY-ON-PAGE before publishing.
5. **International places per medical school + Nigeria-relevant eligibility** (nextgenmedprep/bluepeanut have place counts for 2027 entry but no Nigerian qualification layer).
6. **Graduate-entry medicine for Nigerian degree holders** — zero pages observed.
7. **MDCN recognition / return-to-Nigeria pathway** for UK graduates — one competitor mention, no dedicated page.
8. **Allied courses for Nigerians** (dentistry, physiotherapy, radiography, medical laboratory science/biomedical science) — empty SERPs.
9. **A-level-in-Nigeria provider comparison** (CAIE/Edexcel centres, fees, results) for medicine aspirants — only a directory listicle exists.
10. **Comparison tool / shortlist builder** filtering by qualification route, fee band, international places, UCAT use — none observed.
11. **Resumable application portal with document checklist** (WAEC scratch card/verification, transcripts, SELT, financial evidence for visa) — none observed in results for any competitor.
12. **Nigeria-specific scholarship page with verification status** (listicles exist but are unverified and undated).
13. **Disambiguation content for "MBBS UK Nigeria"** (study in UK vs Nigerian MBBS → GMC/PLAB) — the Nile University news cluster currently dominates.

## 7. Summary

**Top 5 recurring competitor domains (Part A):** theukcatpeople.co.uk (13/25), leadingtuition.co.uk (8), northcypruseducation.com (8), excelsiorscholarships.com (5), gostudyin.com (5) — with abstechconnect.com (5) and studentship.com.ng (4) close behind.

**5 strongest demand signals (INFERENCE):** (1) "Can WAEC/NECO get me into UK medicine?" (forums + consultancies + agent help-centre; contradictory answers); (2) fees/cost for Nigerian students (news, consultancy posts, spam presence); (3) entry requirements by country (four universities maintain Nigeria pages); (4) UCAT logistics in Nigeria (two consultancies flag centre scarcity; no official page); (5) Nigerian doctors → UK (PLAB/housemanship/visa threads; BMA-only SERP) — a different audience to scope deliberately.

**5 clearest gaps (INFERENCE):** (1) per-university WAEC/NECO medicine statements with source + verified date; (2) verified-date fee table in GBP/NGN per school; (3) UCAT-in-Nigeria logistics with official sourcing; (4) "which foundation routes lead to medicine" with eligibility per route; (5) a comparison/shortlist tool and resumable application portal with a Nigeria-specific document checklist — none observed from any competitor.
