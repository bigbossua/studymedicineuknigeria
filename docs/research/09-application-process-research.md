### 2.3 Document upload (new for 2027 entry)

- FACT (UCAS): UCAS has introduced an optional document-upload service for all 2027-entry applicants (it was first available to applicants applying direct to Clearing for 2026 entry after 30 June). Applicants may upload supporting documents to specific sections of the application; documents supplement but do not replace the information entered in the form.
  - SOURCE (applicant guidance): https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/uploading-documents-to-your-application
  - SOURCE (provider guidance): https://www.ucas.com/providers/technical-news-and-developments/provider-developments-at-ucas/document-management-provider-guidance
  - SOURCE (UCAS 2027 adviser toolkit): https://www.ucas.com/advisers/help-and-training/toolkits/2027-cycle-toolkit
- FACT (reported from the UCAS page via snippet): accepted documents include evidence of a previous name, passport or immigration-status document, care-experience information, official qualification certificates, translations, transcripts and English-language certificates. Limits reported: up to five files per upload, maximum 30 documents per application, 5 MB per file; PDF, JPEG, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX, ODT.
- Who can upload: the UCAS applicant page is written for applicants; whether a linked centre/adviser can upload on the applicant's behalf is **NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION** (check the provider/adviser guidance above).
- INFERENCE: this is directly useful for a Nigerian applicant — WAEC/NECO or A-level/IB certificates, transcripts, and an IELTS/other English certificate can now travel with the application instead of being chased by each medical school separately.

### 2.4 Personal statement format (since 2026 entry, continues for 2027)

- FACT (widely reported; UCAS page NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION): the personal statement is three structured questions sharing one 4,000-character limit (including spaces), each answer minimum 350 characters:
  1. Why do you want to study this course or subject?
  2. How have your qualifications and studies helped you to prepare for this course or subject?
  3. What else have you done to prepare outside of education, and why are these experiences useful?
  - LEAD sources: https://www.oxbridgementors.co.uk/blog/ucas-personal-statement-three-questions/ ; https://gradpilot.com/news/ucas-medicine-personal-statement-2027 ; https://bluepeanut.com/ucas-personal-statement-for-medicine/do-medical-schools-read-your-personal-statement-uk-medical-schools-compared-for-2027-entry
  - Confirm wording on ucas.com before publishing.
- The statement is shared across all five choices (FACT, UCAS design).

### 2.5 The four-choice limit for medicine

- FACT (UCAS rule, reported): an applicant may use a maximum of four of the five UCAS choices for medicine (likewise four for dentistry, four for veterinary medicine/science); the fifth choice must be a different course. The limit does not stack across those subjects.
  - SOURCE (UCAS node located, title not resolved): https://ucas.com/node/409161 — VERIFY-ON-PAGE
  - LEAD explanations: https://myrevisionagent.com/guides/how-many-medicine-choices-ucas ; https://bluepeanut.com/medical-school-blog/applying-to-medicine-how-to-choose-a-fifth-ucas-choice
- Common fifth-choice strategies described by LEAD sources: a biomedical/science degree the applicant would genuinely take; a gateway/foundation route where eligible; a related health profession. (Not a UCAS rule — strategy only.)

### 2.6 References

- FACT (UCAS process, reported): the application cannot be submitted without a reference. Two routes:
  - **Through a UCAS-registered centre** (school, college, or registered agent/adviser): the applicant enters the centre's "buzzword", the centre reviews the application, adds predicted grades and the reference, and sends it to UCAS.
  - **Independent applicant**: the applicant enters a referee's details; UCAS emails the referee a secure link to write the reference. Referee must know the applicant academically or professionally; family, friends and partners are not acceptable.
  - SOURCE (UCAS reference processes for advisers): https://www.ucas.com/advisers/help-and-training/guides-resources-and-training/writing-references/reference-processes-and-terminology
  - LEAD summary: https://yourdreamschool.com/ucas-reference-2027-entry-what-to-know/
- Reference structure (three sections since 2024 entry) — specific 2027 wording **NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION**; confirm on the UCAS page above.

### 4.4 2026 test structure (Abstract Reasoning removed)

- FACT (reported; ucat.ac.uk structure page NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION): from 2025 the UCAT has three cognitive subtests — Verbal Reasoning (44 questions / 22 min), Decision Making (35 / 37 min), Quantitative Reasoning (36 / 26 min) — plus Situational Judgement (approx. 69 questions / approx. 26 min). ## 6. Findings — Work experience

- FACT (MSC): the Medical Schools Council's work-experience guidance identifies two useful types of experience — caring/service roles (volunteering or working with people who are ill, disabled or disadvantaged) and observing healthcare (in person **or online**) — and stresses that what matters is what the applicant learned and how they reflect on it, not the prestige of the setting.
  - SOURCE: https://www.medschools.ac.uk/for-students/applying-to-medical-school/work-experience/
- FACT (reported): **Observe GP** (Royal College of General Practitioners) is a free virtual work-experience platform recognised by medical schools as valid relevant experience. (LEAD: https://www.uniadmissions.co.uk/application-guides/observe-gp-virtual-medical-work-experience/ — confirm eligibility for non-UK users on the RCGP page; NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION.)
- University examples of guidance: https://www.christs.cam.ac.uk/study-here/undergraduate-study/subjects/medicine/work-experience ; https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/about/college-of-medicine-and-health/birmingham-medical-school/applying-to-medicine/preparing-to-apply
- School-specific minimums exist (e.g. UCLan reported: two weeks in a medical setting or six months' community volunteering — section 3.2, VERIFY).
- INFERENCE — what a Nigerian applicant can realistically evidence:
  - Shadowing/observation in a Nigerian hospital, primary health centre or private clinic (document dates, setting, supervisor; reflection matters more than duration).
  - Sustained volunteering: care homes/orphanages, disability organisations, community health outreach, NGO clinics, peer tutoring — this maps onto MSC's "caring or service role" category.
  - Virtual NHS exposure: Observe GP and NHS trust virtual programmes (e.g. https://www.qvh.nhs.uk/about-us/work-experience/virtual-work-experience/) — useful because schools want evidence of understanding the NHS specifically.
  - Risks: unpaid "work experience" sold by agents; unverifiable letters. Students should keep a reflective log, since MMI stations probe reflection, not certificates.

## 7. Findings — Offer → deposit → CAS → visa sequence (September 2027 start)

Official anchor points (GOV.UK, via snippets):
- FACT: the earliest a Student visa application can be made from outside the UK is **6 months before the course start date**; a decision is usually given within **3 weeks** from outside the UK. SOURCE: https://www.gov.uk/student-visa
- FACT: the visa application requires a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) issued by the licensed student sponsor once the offer is unconditional and accepted. SOURCE: https://www.gov.uk/student-visa ; UCAS overview: https://www.ucas.com/student-visas-and-immigration-0
- Deposit amounts/rules: university-specific; **NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION** (search budget exhausted before this item). Universities typically require a tuition deposit before issuing a CAS — treat as INFERENCE until each school's page is checked.
- CAS validity period, TB-test requirement for applicants resident in Nigeria, maintenance evidence rules (28-day holding period), and ATAS: **NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION**. (TB testing is a GOV.UK requirement for applicants from listed countries applying for more than 6 months — Nigeria's listing must be confirmed on GOV.UK before publishing.)

INFERENCE — indicative sequence for a September 2027 start:
1. Oct 2026 – Mar 2027: UCAS/direct applications, UCAT scores to schools, interviews.
2. Dec 2026 – May 2027: offers issued (conditional or unconditional). UCAS reply deadlines follow the date of the last decision (dates published by UCAS — NOT FOUND IN THIS SESSION).
3. On firming an offer: pay the international tuition deposit the university requires (amount/timing per university).
4. Summer 2027: results confirmed → offer becomes unconditional → university issues the CAS (CAS cannot be issued until all conditions, deposit and any pre-CAS checks are met).
5. From ~March 2027 at the earliest (6 months before start) but in practice after CAS issue (typically June–August for a September start): Student visa application, with maintenance funds, English evidence, TB certificate (if required), IHS payment, biometrics in Lagos or Abuja.
6. Decision usually within 3 weeks (standard service); travel and enrolment September 2027.
- Risk INFERENCE: the gap between CAS issue and course start is the tightest step for Nigerian applicants (bank-statement holding rules, TB appointment availability, biometric appointment slots). Nothing in the above gives any right to a visa; each decision is UKVI's.
